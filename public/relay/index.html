03 / Ownership & control

A private assistant.
A public explanation.

Relay Internship Assistant is operated by Anant Singhal for his own use. It is not a public email service and does not offer visitor registration or a public Google sign-in.

Anant decides which workflows are enabled and what actions may run automatically. Email and calendar content is treated as information to process, not as authorization to change those rules.

These pages explain the project. They do not expose the private Hermes deployment, Google credentials, inbox, or interview calendar. No Google verification or endorsement is claimed.
