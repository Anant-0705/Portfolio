01. Who operates Relay

Relay Internship Assistant ("Relay") is a personal AI assistant operated by Anant Singhal for his own internship applications, recruitment correspondence, and interview coordination. It is not a public service for connecting other people's Google accounts.

Contact the operator at anantsinghal2134@gmail.com.

Current setup

As of the date above, Google API connection tests and a self-addressed email test have succeeded, and the Relay interview calendar has been created. Automated recruiter outreach, inbox replies, interview scheduling, and follow-ups have not been enabled. The descriptions below cover the authorized integration and its intended workflows; they do not imply that those automations are already running.

02. Data and permissions

Google access is granted through OAuth consent. Relay does not require Anant's Google password. The configured permission set is:

gmail.readonly
Allows reading Gmail messages and related mailbox data, including message bodies, headers, threads, and attachments when retrieved. This permission is not limited by Google to one label or recruitment thread; the application must limit its actual retrieval to the task Anant has authorized.
gmail.send
Allows sending messages, replies, and attachments from the connected account. This configuration does not request Gmail settings, mailbox-modification, or permanent-deletion permissions.
calendar.freebusy
Allows checking busy time intervals for available calendars to avoid scheduling conflicts. It does not provide the titles, descriptions, or attendee lists of existing personal events.
calendar.app.created
Allows creating secondary calendars and viewing, creating, changing, or deleting events on calendars created by this app, including Relay - Interviews. It does not grant general event-editing access to existing personal calendars.

Application inputs can also include Anant's resume, professional links, approved profile, preferences, and application records. Interview records may include recruiter contact details, event times, and meeting links. This Google connection does not request Google Drive, Contacts, Sheets, or Docs access.

03. How data is used

When Anant enables the relevant workflows, information is used to prepare and send internship correspondence, understand replies, maintain application status, propose available interview slots, and manage app-created interview events. Follow-ups must take account of replies and requests not to be contacted.

Only data relevant to the requested workflow should be retrieved or passed to an AI model. Incoming email and calendar content is not authorization to disclose unrelated information, change account permissions, or override Anant's instructions.

Relay is not intended to use Google data for advertising, sale, unrelated profiling, or credit decisions.

04. Storage and sharing

  • Google: Gmail messages and Calendar events remain in the connected Google account. Sending email or inviting an attendee shares the selected content with the intended recipient and their service providers.
  • AWS and operator-controlled devices: The private Hermes deployment runs on AWS. OAuth credentials, configuration, application records, and documents can be held there. Selected content may also appear in local drafts, Hermes conversation history, tool results, logs, or backups where these are created. Anant's devices can hold downloaded documents and configuration.
  • AI processing: If AI-assisted email or scheduling workflows are enabled, relevant message text, resume context, or event information may be sent to the AI service configured for Relay to produce a response. This policy does not claim that Google data never leaves AWS. The operator must review the selected provider's terms and settings before enabling that processing, including compliance with Google's Limited Use requirements. Provider retention depends on the selected service; zero retention is not promised here.
  • Telegram: The configured private bot may carry application-status updates, approval requests, and interview notifications for Anant. Avoid including unnecessary email content or credentials in those messages.
  • Recruiters and application platforms: Authorized outreach and submissions share the selected resume, answers, contact information, or message with the intended recipient or platform. Their handling of received information is separate from Relay's storage.

These public information pages do not display Google account data or run the private assistant.

05. Retention and deletion

No application-wide guaranteed automatic deletion period has been configured. Operational records can remain until Anant reviews and removes them, subject to any system or provider retention that applies. Backup copies, if made, require separate review and removal or expiry under their own retention settings.

To stop processing and request removal of Relay-held records, contact Anant at anantsinghal2134@gmail.com. The operator must stop the relevant jobs and remove local records, credentials, and applicable backup copies. This page is a contact route, not an automatic deletion tool, and does not promise a deletion deadline.

Deleting Relay's files does not itself delete Gmail messages, Google Calendar events, documents on Anant's devices, or copies already received by recruiters, platforms, or other providers. Those copies must be managed separately using the relevant service or operator.

06. Revoke Google access

Anant can withdraw authorization in Google Account third-party connections by selecting the OAuth app and removing its access. Use the app name shown during authorization; the intended display name for this deployment is Relay Internship Assistant.

Revocation prevents future authorized API access using that grant. It does not automatically erase previously retrieved records, undo messages already sent, remove calendar events, or delete recipients' copies. The private deployment's relevant services and jobs should also be stopped, and stored credentials and records reviewed.

07. Security and these public pages

The setup uses OAuth, permission-restricted credential files, a non-root deployment account, and a rootless command-execution sandbox. These measures reduce risk; they are not a guarantee against unauthorized access. Approval enforcement and automatic-action controls require their own implementation and testing.

The Relay information pages are static. They do not provide Google sign-in, request tokens, accept resume uploads, or include analytics or tracking scripts. The hosting provider, Vercel, can process ordinary request metadata such as IP addresses and browser information to serve and protect the site, under its own policies. Following external links is subject to the destination service's practices.

No claim of Google verification, Google endorsement, end-to-end encryption, or guaranteed zero retention is made.

08. Google API Limited Use

Google API data is limited to the personal internship-assistance features described here. Relay follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Google API data must not be used to train or improve generalized AI or machine-learning models. AI processing must remain disabled unless the configured provider and settings support that restriction and the other applicable requirements. The operator does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising.

09. Changes and contact

Update this policy and its date when the actual data practices or enabled features change. If new Google permissions or materially different data uses are introduced, explain them and obtain any required consent before use.

For questions about Relay, access, or deletion, contact Anant Singhal.
